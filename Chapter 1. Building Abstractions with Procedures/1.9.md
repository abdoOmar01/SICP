```scm
(define (+ a b)
    (if (= a 0) b (inc (+ (dec a) b))))
```

```scm
(+ 4 5)
(inc (+ 3 5))
(inc (inc (+ 2 5)))
(inc (inc (inc (+ 1 5))))
(inc (inc (inc (inc (+ 0 5)))))
(inc (inc (inc (inc 5))))
(inc (inc (inc 6)))
(inc (inc 7))
(inc 8)
9
```

```scm
(define (+ a b)
    (if (= a 0) b (+ (dec a) (inc b))))
```

```scm
(+ 4 5)
(+ 3 6)
(+ 2 7)
(+ 1 8)
(+ 0 9)
9
```

The first process is recursive, as its shape is a series of expansion (deferred operations) followed by contractions (the operations being performed). The second process is iterative, as each step can be continued from by supplying the state variables.
